Say "13 rows have an empty `accounts.sources`", not "13 accounts have no
restriction". **Why:** a claim that launders a column into a behaviour hides its
own gap. **How to apply:** name the field you actually read. See
[[reference_build_takes_nine_minutes]] for the sibling habit on timings.
